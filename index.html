Meaning

“Random” is a slang word that means something or someone unexpected, strange, or appearing for no clear reason. In casual English, it’s often used when something seems unusual or unrelated to what’s happening.

Etymology

Once, the word random meant “swift motion,” from the Old French randon — derived from randir (“to rush, to run”). Later, the English borrowed it, and its meaning shifted — from “force and haste” to “chance and disorder.” That’s how random became the symbol of everything unpredictable.

Usage

The word “random” is used to describe something unexpected, accidental, or without logic. It fits situations or events that happen without any clear plan or reason. In slang, random often means weird or absurd in a surprising way. For example: “We met him in another city — that was so random!”
